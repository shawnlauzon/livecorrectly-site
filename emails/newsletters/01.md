Hi {{firstName}},

Admit it. Even those of you living in 5D / 7D / 9D still spend time eating and sleeping.

Human Design is not about pulling you out of the Matrix. It’s about recognizing that you are in the Matrix (we typically call it the Maia) and how to use that to our advantage. How can we align with these forms which we inhabit and so have success, satisfaction, peace, and surprise.

So I thought it would be fun to match the characters in one of my favorite movies to the career types. This can serve as a good intro to the types as well!

Spoiler alerts :)

## The Initator

![Morpheus]({{appUrl}}/newsletter/morpheus.png)

Morpheus is our Initiator (Manifestor in traditional Human Design). He gets things started. He actively looks for the right people and invites them to be on his crew. He doesn’t wait for anyone: he has a vision for the future and acts to make it happen.

## The Builder

![Neo]({{appUrl}}/newsletter/neo.jpeg)

Neo is our Builder (or Generator). He has lots of energy Before his awakening he had a normal job, and then what he loved which is being a hacker and searching for answers. He wasn’t trying to be seen: people came to him. Including Morpheus who recognized him as the One and invited him. He said no until he saw the White Rabbit and responded to it. Especially in the first movie, he is for the most part just responding to what others are doing / saying.

## The Advisor

![The Oracle]({{appUrl}}/newsletter/oracle.avif)

For our Advisor (or Projector) we have the Oracle. The Oracle relaxes at her home, doing what she enjoys (making cookies), and people come to her one on one and she gives them advice. She isn’t advertising her business; people recognize her because of her mastery, and then they seek her out.

## The Evaluator

![The Matrix characters]({{appUrl}}/newsletter/matrix01.jpg)

Unfortunately there’s no one who clearly fits our Evaluator. Which is ironic because Evaluators have the gift to recognize who are the “special” ones, those that don’t exactly fit in and are meant for something more. So in the movie Morpheus takes on this role as well, but as a much more active role than the typical Evaluator. And since Evaluators are only 1% of the population, it’s enough to leave it there.
