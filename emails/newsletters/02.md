Those two dreaded words in your decision-making strategy:

**Wait for…**

90% of people are designed to wait.

- There are the Builders: **Wait for the response**
- And the Advisors: **Wait for the invitation**
- And the Evaluators: **Wait for a 28-day cycle**
  (which sounds absolutely crazy, but at 1% of the population I don’t have enough experience to talk to it).

And yes, if you think of waiting as sitting at home in your PJ’s eating Nutella waiting for a text, you’ll feel…

Bitter. Disappointed. Frustrated.

Thankfully, that’s not what waiting means. At least, it’s not what _actively waiting_ means, which is what we’re talking about here.

## Actively waiting means not forcing things

Do the things that you want to do. If you want to share on social media, do it! If you want to stay at home and get better at your specific skill, do it! Live your life doing what feels good to you.

I’ll share more per-type suggestions at the bottom.

One thing I’ve noticed from experience: anytime my mind says that I **\*should** do something*, it usually means forcing: I don’t want to do it, but the story is that if I *don’t\* do it, then no one will ever see me.

And I’ve discovered this isn’t true. When I do what my mind says I _should_ do, I end up feeling frustrated and eventually quit. But when I wait for what my gut says yes to, I keep going because I feel satisfied. I’m doing what I _want_ to do, and that is sustainable. And I trust that people will see me when the time is right.

That _does_ mean that you need to be seen at some point. Maybe not now (especially for you Advisors meant to master a system!), but eventually for sure. You’ll know when the time is right.

There’s a massive difference between just living your life and being seen versus _trying_ to be seen. Being thirsty: it’s not a good look for love or life.

## But Shawn, what if I’m broke and can’t wait?

Yeah, the struggle is real. In my experience if you’re feeling anxious and are struggling to have enough money to meet basic needs, then following your design may be too hard right now. You can still practice with it, especially with less critical decisions, but avoid using it to make financial decisions. After all, your mind is strong, and this is a fight you can’t win until you learn to trust your decision-making strategy.
