Hi {{firstName}},

Besides Human Design, Authentic Relating is my other practice which changed my life. It's a framework for deep, meaningful conversations that helps you show up as your true self in every relationship. It pairs quite nicely with Human Design, in that both of them are pointing to an allowing which starts with yourself and expands to others and the world.

Too often, we fight against the reality of the world. We try to believe that the world is not how we think it should be. We ignore evidence that contrasts with these beliefs.

Related to this is the never-ending journey of self-improvement, which most recently I see as a craving to "heal" something. The belief is that you are broken in some way, and that only by changing yourself can you be happy.

And how do we determine that we're broken? By comparing to others who are "normal" (aka "ideal"), and then when notice that we're different, try to fix.

It's the same as wanting to "conform", but that has a negative connotation so we call it "self-improvement" or "healing" or "growth".

### The other way

Practice number 1 in Authentic Relating is Welcome Everything. It's a radical concept. Rather than seeing something that we perceive is "bad", we see it exactly as it is. We don't make it worse, we don't make it better. Just seeing that it is here. And accept that is the truth.

The mind will rebel against this. The mind always likes to push things away or bring things closer, to change the world into what it believes it wants. This gives it something to do.
