Hey {{firstName}},

I've done my share of esoteric practices. I first learned about Human Design while on a yoga retreat in Thailand back in 2015. I participated in my first ayahuasca session the same year. It was the time of my 40 year cycle, and makes sense that a lot of things were happening then. And I had been meditating for a long time before that.

And all through this time, I saw that people (including myself) have wanted to escape from this mundane life.

Sure there's something to be said about being in a nice, relaxed state: it's hard to [follow your decision-making strategy]({{chartUrl}}) if you're feeling anxious. But just going to a single event won't separate you from your daily stressors. Even when an event, meditation, or medicine journey brings you to an elevated state, you'll still be in the Matrix.

Human Design is different. It's not about pulling you out of the Matrix. It’s about recognizing who you are _within_ the Matrix (what we call the Maia), and how to use that to your advantage. We learn to align with our unique form which we inhabit to increase our levels of success, satisfaction, peace, and surprise.

Since The Matrix is one of my favorite movies, I thought it would be fun to describe each of the four Human Design types with characters from the movie. It's also a great intro to Human Design if you're new to the system! You may not ever leave the Matrix, but with this knowledge maybe you can dodge bullets! (probably not)

Spoiler alert :)

## The Initiator

![Image of Morpheus from The Matrix]({{appUrl}}/newsletter/morpheus.png)

Morpheus is our Initiator (Manifestor in traditional Human Design). He gets things started. He actively looks for the right people and invites them to be on his crew. He doesn’t wait for anyone: he has a vision for the future and acts to make it happen. And although Manifestors can wade into the battle, they are a better fit to allow others to do the daily work. Around 10% of the population are Initiators. Which brings us to ...

## The Builder

![Image of Neo from The Matrix]({{appUrl}}/newsletter/neo.jpeg)

Neo is our Builder (or Generator). The energy of Builders (around 65% of the population) continually regenerates and so are designed to work. Before his awakening, Neo had a normal job, but after getting home he still had energy to do what he loved: being a hacker and searching for answers. While searching he came to the attention of Morpheus, who recognized him as the One and invited him to his cause. And when he saw the White Rabbit, he immediately responded to it. Especially in the first movie, he is for the most part just responding to what others are doing / saying. But he struggled until meeting ...

![Image of the Oracle from The Matrix]({{appUrl}}/newsletter/oracle.avif)

For our Advisor (or Projector) we have the Oracle. She relaxes at her home, doing what she enjoys (making cookies), and people come to her one by one for advice. She isn’t advertising her business—people recognize her because of her mastery, and then seek her out. With their penetrating aura, Advisors can literally feel the other person. ## The Evaluator

![Image of the Architect from The Matrix]({{appUrl}}/newsletter/architect.jpg)

The Architect from the 2nd Matrix movie is our Evaluator (or Reflector). Although he did build the initial system, he has a more important role: knowing the health of the system from a bird's eye view (as all the video monitors make clear). From this place, Evaluators can both understand how thigns are going, as well as noticing specific people (like the One!) who are somehow different from the rest. They are extremely rare: only 1% of the population!
