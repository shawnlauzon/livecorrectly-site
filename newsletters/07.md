## For Builders

Your energy resources are continually being regenerated. You are designed to work until you are exhausted, go to sleep, and then wake up and do it again. As a Builder myself, I know that when I'm doing what I love I can keep going and going

![Energizer Bunny]({{appUrl}}/newsletter/energizer-bunny.jpg)
_Builders are designed to keep going and going_

But there is a caveat: this only works when you are doing what you love. If you're being pressured by others to do work that you don't want to do, then the energy is not regenerated. In fact, you'll go home completely exhausted, although the next day you will probably be in reasonably good shape.

### Short-term solution: Find the parts you love

Your issue is never not having enough energy—it's not loving all of it. So either focus on the parts of the work that you love and let others do the rest, or find the joy in even the parts you would rather not do. Maybe you can be satisfied by optimizing a part of the work. And if you find yourself frustated, pause for a moment to see if there's anything which can be changed.

## For Advisors

However if you are an Advisor, being pushed like this can be devastating. Here's an Advisor client of mine who has been dealing with this for her entire career and is looking for a way out:

> I was exhausted... I've been doing this for 16 years. Not like I don't know what I'm doing, but I hate it. And every semester I have a big meltdown ... I come home and I have to be in a dark room and close my eyes because it's too much.

While Builders who are pushed like this can get by for awhile, for Advisors it can create physical injury. Ever since I remember, my Advisor father has worked himself to the bone, never knowing when enough is enough. And as a result, he appears literally 20 years older than my mother, who is in fact a bit older than him (love you Dad!)

### Short-term solution: Stop working _before_ you are tired

While Builders know when it's time to stop, Advisors usually don't have as good of a feeling for themselves. So it's important to stop—or at least take long breaks—before feeling tired. If you wait until you notice you're tired, it will be exhaustion for sure.

## For Initiators

All Initiators have a defined motor, and so they are able to push themselves harder than many Advisors. However, this can also come at the risk of health issues if they don't safeguard their energy.

It's true, my Initiator clients typically have other issues (usually about misreading people), and they are also designed to invite other people to do the work and so it comes more naturally! But if you are feeling burnout, it's important to delegate where possible to others.

![Man tending a grill]({{appUrl}}/newsletter/man-on-grill.jpg)
_Initiators are designed to get things started and then step back_

### Short-term solution: Delegate

You're designed to invite others to do work, and so try to do more of that. You can see great opportunities around you, and doing the work yourself can exhaust you to the point where you're not able to do your magic. And others are literally waiting for you to invite them.
