## Your body keeps the score (and knows which way to go)

**First**: You need to start paying attention to what your body is telling you.

This is not what most Human Design people will tell you about. They'll go on and on about what this gate means and what that channel means. But none of it matters if you can't put your attention on your body.

This was a struggle for me. As a smart, clumsy, socially awkward kid who loved computers, my life was my brain. When I drew a picture of myself, it was a big head and a little body. No concept of anything else.

And so it was hard to start paying attention to everything my body told me. Karla McLaren's book _The Language of Emotions_ opened a whole world, that anger was not meant to be suppressed (thanks Catholicism), but to be listened to because _it's tell us something_.

Years later, Authentic Relating practice #1 _Welcome everything_ goes even further: whatever comes, don't try to change it (at least not initially). First accept the reality of the situation (especially devious thought: "It shouldn't be like that". Yeah buddy, but it is)

All of this is to say: your body knows a lot, and it is constantly talking to you. Listen to it.

### Exercise: Two minute feeling

If you find this difficult, here's a two minute exercise:

1. Turn off your phone, music, TV, anything that you can see or hear.
2. Close your eyes
3. Take 3 deep breaths
4. Now, put your attention on your stomach. As it rises and falls, simply notice it.
5. Continue to notice it for one minute. Notice how your stomach gets bigger and smaller. Notice if it changes, getting faster or slower.
6. Congratulate yourself on your first step to

How did it go? Anything surprising?

## Stop with the Shoulds

Awesome, your body is going to be your new best friend. Because you'll need them as an ally against THE MIND.

No your mind isn't out to get you, but it sure acts like that sometimes.

Why? Because it's trying to keep you safe. And it keeps you safe by playing small. It keeps you safe by doing the same thing you've been doing for the past 20, 30, 40-some years because, well you're still alive, and so it can't be THAT bad!

It's also the spokesperson for what's called the Not-Self. Remember way back in the first email, I told you about your #1 shadow? Well, it's your mind that keeps you there. If you're like me and have the shadow of _Touchy & nervous_, it's your mind which will tell you _you shouldn't go_ to that event because "you don't know anyone" or "no one will like you there".

See, it's keeping you safe by avoiding anything that might be uncomfortable.

And which might be the thing to give you an outstanding life.

Those _Shoulds_ pop up all over the place: "you shouldn't tell them what you think" (because they might not like or) or "you should go to that boring even you said yes to" (because if you don't, then people won't like you).

It's not like your mind is always lying to you. But it's certainly not telling you the truth when it concerns YOU.

The mind is great at what is happening outside your self. But inside? That's where the Body rules.

### Exercise: Ask your body what to do

And here's where the decision making strategy comes in which I've talked about previously. Your mind is going to tell you to avoid something. And rather than listen to it, listen to what your body says:

1. Do the two-minute feeling exercise above. This will connect you with your body

## For Builders

Your energy resources are continually being regenerated. You are designed to work until you are exhausted, go to sleep, and then wake up and do it again. As a Builder myself, I know that when I'm doing what I love I can keep going and going

![Energizer Bunny]({{appUrl}}/newsletter/energizer-bunny.jpg)
_Builders are designed to keep going and going—if they're doing what they love_

But there is a caveat: this only works when you are doing what you love. If you're being pressured by others to do work that you don't want to do, then the energy is not regenerated. In fact, you'll go home completely exhausted, although the next day you will probably be in reasonably good shape.

### Short-term solution: Find the parts you love

Your issue is never not having enough energy—it's not loving all of it. So either focus on the parts of the work that you love and let others do the rest, or find the joy in even the parts you would rather not do. Maybe you can be satisfied by optimizing a part of the work. And if you find yourself frustrated, pause for a moment to see if there's anything which can be changed.

## For Advisors

However if you are an Advisor, being pushed like this can be devastating. Here's an Advisor client of mine who has been dealing with this for her entire career and is looking for a way out:

> I was exhausted... I've been doing this for 16 years. Not like I don't know what I'm doing, but I hate it. And every semester I have a big meltdown ... I come home and I have to be in a dark room and close my eyes because it's too much.

While Builders who are pushed like this can get by for awhile, for Advisors it can create physical injury. Ever since I remember, my Advisor father has worked himself to the bone, never knowing when enough is enough. And as a result, he appears literally 20 years older than my mother, who is in fact a bit older than him (love you Dad!)

### Short-term solution: Stop working _before_ you are tired

While Builders know when it's time to stop, Advisors usually don't have as good of a feeling for themselves. So it's important to stop—or at least take long breaks—before feeling tired. If you wait until you notice you're tired, it will be exhaustion for sure.

## For Initiators

All Initiators have a defined motor, and so they are able to push themselves harder than many Advisors. However, this can also come at the risk of health issues if they don't safeguard their energy.

It's true, my Initiator clients typically have other issues (usually about misreading people), and they are also designed to invite other people to do the work and so it comes more naturally! But if you are feeling burnout, it's important to delegate where possible to others.

![Man tending a grill]({{appUrl}}/newsletter/man-on-grill.jpg)
_Initiators are designed to get things started and then step back_

### Short-term solution: Delegate

You're designed to invite others to do work, and so try to do more of that. You can see great opportunities around you, and doing the work yourself can exhaust you to the point where you're not able to do your magic. And others are literally waiting for you to invite them.
